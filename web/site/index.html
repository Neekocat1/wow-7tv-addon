7TV emote pack builder

for the 7TV Emotes addon in World of Warcraft: Forever. Pick a streamer, download a zip, drop it in your AddOns folder. Nothing to install.

    Install your pack

    1. Install the 7TV Emotes addon (from CurseForge, choose the Forever version) if you haven't already.
    2. Open World of Warcraft\_forever_\Interface\AddOns. If there's an old SevenTVEmotes_Local folder, delete it first.
    3. Extract the zip there, so you get AddOns\SevenTVEmotes_Local.
    4. Restart WoW (a /reload isn't enough for new image files). Type /7tv to see your emotes.

    To change your emotes later, build a new pack and replace the folder the same way.

    Good to know

    • Want to add emotes from inside the game instead? Use the free 7TV Emotes Helper app for Windows: it keeps your emotes up to date and adds new ones with /7tv add. If you use the helper, you don't need packs (it replaces the pack's folder).
    • Everything happens in your browser: it downloads the emotes from 7TV and converts them on your computer. Nothing is uploaded or stored anywhere else.
    • Only 7TV emotes are supported, not BetterTTV or FrankerFaceZ. Many of those are on 7TV too: add them by name above.
    • Zero-width (overlay) emotes are skipped. Emotes belong to their creators on 7TV. This project isn't affiliated with 7TV, Twitch or Blizzard.
